# Locate a host's cancellation policy

Cancellation details are shown on the listing and during booking. For a confirmed reservation, the guest can find the policy in Trips under Reservation details. Use this document as a retrieval-routing rule when an exact refund depends on a reservation-specific policy.

## Find the cancellation policy on a desktop

Click the listing you want to check
Go to Things to know
Under Cancellation policy, find if there’s a free cancellation period
Click Show more to get more details about what’s refunded if you cancel

## Find the cancellation policy for a reservation on desktop 
Click Trips and select the reservation you want to check
Under Reservation details, go to Cancellation policy
Click Read more
