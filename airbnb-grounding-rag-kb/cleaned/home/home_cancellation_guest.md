# Understanding home cancellation policies as a guest

Home cancellation policies are set by hosts and vary by listing. A fully refundable reservation can be cancelled before its specified deadline; some listings are non-refundable or partially refundable after certain dates. The reservation-specific policy can be found in reservation details. Exact refund questions therefore require the booking's applicable policy.
