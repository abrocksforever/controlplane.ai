# Understanding home cancellation policies as a guest

For home reservations, cancellation policies are set by the host and vary by listing. For certain homes, eligible guests can extend the free cancellation window to 24 hours before check-in by paying more for the reservation—the additional reservation cost is paid to Airbnb.

## What happens when you cancel your home reservation

If your reservation has a fully refundable policy, you can cancel for free before the specified date and time. Keep in mind that some listings might have non-refundable or only partially refundable policies after certain dates, so free cancellation wouldn't be an option.

Find out more about how to cancel your home reservation as a guest.

## Where to find the cancellation policy

You can find the cancellation policy displayed on the listing page, when you confirm and pay, and in your confirmation email, so you'll know exactly what to expect if your plans change.

## Cancellation policies may change for specific dates

Hosts can adjust cancellation policies for specific dates. The cancellation policy for your stay is based on the check-in date, not how many nights you book

## Altering an existing home reservation

Before you send a trip change request, if the cancellation policy changes, you’ll be able to review the updated cancellation policy and refund timeline. The host must approve any change.

Here’s what could happen:

Check-in date change: A different cancellation policy may apply
Only checkout date changes: The cancellation policy remains unchanged

## Refunds and timing

If you start the cancellation process from your Trips you can get a detailed breakdown of your refund before you confirm. If you’re eligible for a refund, we’ll initiate your refund as soon as you cancel.

Refunds follow the cancellation policy that applies when you cancel
Airbnb processes refunds right away; your bank controls how soon the money posts
If a host cancels, you get a full refund and help finding a similar place, no matter the policy
