# Cancellation policies for your home

Home hosts can choose cancellation policies for short-term and long-term/monthly stays. Current shorter-stay examples include Flexible (full refund until 24 hours before check-in), Moderate (5 days), Limited (14 days with partial-refund rules), and Firm (30 days with partial-refund rules). Some Strict/Super Strict options are invitation-only. Standard shorter-stay policies include a 24-hour cancellation period after confirmation when the reservation was confirmed at least 7 days before check-in. Monthly stays of 28+ nights use separate long-term policies. The listing's local time zone controls deadlines. A policy can be overridden by qualifying Major Disruptive Events or covered home Reservation Issues.
