# Change your home cancellation policy

Hosts can edit a home's cancellation policy when the option is available. Changes apply only to future reservations and do not affect pending or confirmed reservations. The policy in force when an existing reservation was received continues to apply.

## Edit your cancellation policy on desktop

Click Listings and select the listing you want to edit
Under Listing editor, click Your space
Click Cancellation policy
Next to your policy, click Edit
Make your selection and then click Save

## What happens to the cancellation policy for existing reservations when you make a change

Any changes you make to your cancellation policy will only apply to future reservations and won’t affect any pending or confirmed reservations. The cancellation policy that was set when you received a reservation will still apply to that reservation.

## Set short-term stay cancellation policies directly from your calendar

You can set different short-term stay cancellation policies to specific date ranges right from your calendar. This gives you the flexibility to protect earnings during peak periods and offer more flexible terms when demand isn’t as high. These dynamic policies are totally optional–if you don't set any, your main listing policy will just stay in effect.

## Listing eligibility for an extended cancellation option

Certain listings with moderate, limited, firm, or strict cancellation policies are automatically eligible to offer an extended cancellation option to guests. If your listing has a different policy, or you’re not eligible, you won’t have this option. Offering an extended cancellation option protects your earnings while giving guests flexibility.

If you don’t want to have an extended cancellation option for guests, you can opt out at any time.
