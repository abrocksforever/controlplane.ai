# Cancel your home reservation as a guest

Guests cancel home reservations through Trips and Reservation details. The refund is governed by the applicable cancellation policy unless an exception or overriding policy applies. Airbnb states the refund cannot exceed the amount actually paid. After check-in, a guest who cancels is expected to leave the property. Problems during the stay may invoke a separate Reservation Issue process.
