# Cancel your home reservation as a guest

Guests cancel home reservations through Trips and Reservation details. The refund is governed by the applicable cancellation policy unless an exception or overriding policy applies. Airbnb states the refund cannot exceed the amount actually paid. After check-in, a guest who cancels is expected to leave the property. Problems during the stay may invoke a separate Reservation Issue process.

## Cancel a reservation on desktop

Click Trips and select the reservation you want to cancel
Under Reservation details, click Cancel reservation
Choose a reason for cancelling
Click Cancel reservation

## Cancellation policies for home stays

For home reservations, cancellation policies are set by the host and vary by listing. If a host has selected a policy with a fully refundable option, you can cancel the reservation for free – just make sure you cancel before the time and date listed. Cancellation deadlines for receiving refunds are measured from the check-in time for the listing in its local time zone or 15:00 if no check-in time is specified.

Keep in mind that some listings may be non-refundable, or only partially refundable, after certain dates and times. In these cases, you won’t be able to cancel your reservation for free. In the rare circumstances that a large-scale event at your destination prevents completion of your reservation, you may be eligible for a refund under Airbnb’s Major Disruptive Events Policy.

## How cancelling a home reservation affects service reservations

If you have other services booked at the home you’re cancelling, you’ll need to cancel them separately or make other arrangements with your service host.

## Check if you’ll receive a refund before cancelling
Learn more about what’s refunded when you cancel a home stay reservation. Remember that the amount refunded will never be more than the amount you have actually paid at the time of cancelling. You can find your refund amount before or after cancelling your reservation.

## If you cancel after check-in

If you cancel your reservation after you’ve already checked in, you’re expected to leave the property immediately.

## If you’re cancelling because of an issue during your stay

If you’re experiencing an issue during your stay, you can ask your host to fix it, request a partial refund or request to cancel your reservation for a full refund.

Before cancelling, find out what your options are if there’s an issue during your stay

## What happens if you have travel insurance or Stay Protection

If you purchased travel insurance or Stay Protection for your reservation, you may be covered for some costs relating to your cancellation. To file a claim with your insurer, first check your policy. You should have received an email with details about the process for cancellations that:

Confirms your insurance purchase
Provides a copy of your insurance policy
Includes a link to your policy details page
On your policy details page, you can cancel your policy, start a claim and access other services.

## How to cancel a service or experience

Cancelling a service or experience? You can usually cancel for free up to 1 day (24 hours) before the start time of the service or experience, but certain services and experiences allow you to cancel for a full refund up to 3 days (72 hours) before the start time.
