# Weather Events, Natural Conditions and Diseases Excluded from the Major Disruptive Events Policy

The [**Major Disruptive Events Policy**](https://www.airbnb.co.in/help/article/1320/) (the “Policy”) explains how Airbnb handles cancellations and refunds when large-scale events prevent or legally prohibit completion of a reservation.

The Policy excludes weather or natural conditions that are common enough to be foreseeable in a given location, except when they result in another covered Event that prevents completion of the reservation, such as a mandatory evacuation order or large-scale outage of essential utilities.

Below is a **non-exhaustive list** of common foreseeable weather events, natural conditions and endemic diseases. This list may change over time.

Guests are encouraged to research their destination before booking and carefully review their reservation’s cancellation policy.
