# Weather events, natural conditions, and diseases that may be excluded

The Major Disruptive Events Policy excludes weather or natural conditions that are common enough to be foreseeable in a location. Such conditions can become relevant if they result in another covered event, such as a mandatory evacuation or large-scale essential-utility outage. The exclusions are non-exhaustive and may change.
