# Refunds in India

For Indian residents, Airbnb states that Indian credit-card authentication can result in the full reservation amount being charged when a trip request is submitted. If the host does not accept the request or it expires, Airbnb says the guest is refunded in full and Airbnb initiates the refund immediately. Banks/providers may take up to 15 business days. Listed timelines include UPI 5–7 business days, net banking 10–15, RuPay 10–15, Google Pay up to 15, and Razorpay up to 10 days. Confirmed duplicate requests are not automatically refundable and remain subject to the host's cancellation policy.
