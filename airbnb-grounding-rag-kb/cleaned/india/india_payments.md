# Paying for reservations in India

For Indian residents using Indian credit cards, Airbnb says the full reservation cost can be charged when submitting a trip request because of local two-factor authentication requirements. Once booked, Airbnb says the payment method cannot be changed. This behavior should not be generalized to other regions or payment methods.
