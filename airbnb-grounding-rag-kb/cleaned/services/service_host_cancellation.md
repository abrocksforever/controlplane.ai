# Host Cancellation Policy for Services and Experiences

Hosts are expected to honor confirmed service/experience reservations. Airbnb may impose a cancellation fee and other consequences; the current policy states a potential fee of 20% of booking value excluding taxes and other fees. Fees may be waived for valid reasons beyond the host's control, with evidence where required. A host can be responsible when the service differs materially from the booking, including failing to start within 15 minutes. This policy applies to services/experiences, not homes.
