# Cancellation policies for service and experience listings

Most services and experiences have a 1-day policy: full refund until 24 hours before the local start time. Certain offerings have a 3-day policy: full refund until 72 hours before local start time. The 3-day option is only available to certain hosts. Guests can review the reservation-specific policy. Major Disruptive Events and Covered Issues can override the listing policy.

For certain services and experiences, hosts will have the option to choose a 3-day cancellation policy when setting up or managing their listing. This means guests can cancel for a full refund until 72 hours before the service or experience start time (at the local time of the service or experience), and the host won’t be paid for that booking. This policy option is only available to certain hosts depending on the specifications of their service or experience.

Guests can review the host’s cancellation policy when they book a service or experience.

# When the listing cancellation policy may be overridden for a guest refund

There are certain circumstances in which a guest might be entitled to a refund regardless of the cancellation policy for the service or experience. For example, if a large-scale event at the location of the listing prevents or legally prohibits completion of the reservation, the Major Disruptive Events Policy will apply and a guest may cancel for a full refund. The host won’t get a payout for that booking, regardless of the listing cancellation policy.

The listing cancellation policy may also be overridden if the host is responsible for a Covered Issue under our Refund Policy for Services and Experiences.

If a host is paid for the reservation and their cancellation policy is overridden, Airbnb may recover any money owed by the host as provided in our Payments Terms of Service. An adjustment will be automatically deducted from the next scheduled payout.

Our final decisions relating to cancellations and refunds do not affect other contractual or statutory rights that may be available to you. Any right that guests or hosts may have to initiate legal action remains unaffected.
