# Refund Policy for Services and Experiences

A Covered Issue can make a guest eligible for a full or partial refund. Covered Issues include host cancellation except for guest-caused disruption; host being more than 15 minutes late or not arriving; substantial difference from what was advertised; or host not being prepared to host. If the host cancels for a non-guest-caused reason, the guest automatically receives a full refund. Other refunds depend on severity and impact.
