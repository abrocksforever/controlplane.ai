# How you'll get your refund

Eligible refunds are issued to the original payment method. If the original account is closed, the guest may need to work with the bank. Airbnb says eligible refunds are initiated as soon as cancellation occurs, but receipt time depends on the bank or financial institution. Credits may sometimes be chosen instead of a refund to the original payment method.

# Refunds are issued to your original payment method

Refunds are issued to the original payment method that you used when you made the reservation. This applies even if you split the payment over multiple payment methods. In many cases, you’ll notice both a payment charge and a separate refund amount on your payment method.

# If your original payment method can’t be refunded

If the account of your original payment method has been closed, the refund sent by Airbnb won’t be processed. If this happens, you can contact your bank or financial institution to locate it. If you currently have an account with them, they may be able to transfer the refund to your new card or account. In some cases, they may send you a cheque for the refunded amount instead.

If you need to provide them with the refund details, you can contact Airbnb – we can provide you with the details, including a reference number that your bank or financial institution can use.

# If you cancel within 24 hours of booking

If you cancel within the first 24 hours before the reservation is confirmed, we may not have charged your payment quite yet and only placed a hold on your payment method. In this case, we’ll simply remove the payment hold, which is often an invisible backend process. However, if your reservation has already been accepted, the refund will be subject to the host’s cancellation policy.

# If you paid with booking credit

Refunds for booking credit are subject to your host’s cancellation policy and may be refunded as credit that can be used again. Read more about how booking credit works.

# If you paid with a coupon

If you cancel a reservation in which you used a coupon to pay, the coupon can’t be used again.

If your host cancels a reservation in which you used a coupon to pay, your coupon code will remain active and can be used towards another reservation. Find out more about what happens to the coupon you used if your host cancels.

# Choosing credits instead of a refund

When you cancel, you may have the option to choose credits instead of a refund to your original payment method. This means that credits will be applied to your Airbnb account and you won’t receive a refund to your original payment method.

# How long refunds take

Eligible refunds will be initiated by Airbnb as soon as you cancel the reservation, but how long it takes for you to receive the money depends on your bank or financial institution. Find the average refund timelines.
