# Canceling during your stay / reporting reservation issues

Guests generally have 72 hours from discovery to report a problem with a home, service, or experience to the host or Airbnb. Evidence such as photos or videos should be gathered. For home issues, the Rebooking and refund policy for homes is relevant. A qualifying Major Disruptive Event can provide another refund path.
