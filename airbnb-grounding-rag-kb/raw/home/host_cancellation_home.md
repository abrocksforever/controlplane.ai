# Host Cancellation Policy for homes

Official source: https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/990
Retrieved: 2026-08-29

A home host who cancels a confirmed reservation or is found responsible for a cancellation may face fees and other consequences. Fees/consequences can be waived in certain valid circumstances, including qualifying Major Disruptive Events or reasons beyond the host's control. This policy is specific to homes.
