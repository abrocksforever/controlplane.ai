# Major Disruptive Events Policy

Official source: https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/1320
Retrieved: 2026-08-29

The Major Disruptive Events Policy may apply when a large-scale event prevents or legally prohibits completion of a reservation. It applies to homes, services, and experiences. When applicable, guests may cancel and receive a refund, travel credit, and/or other consideration regardless of the normal cancellation policy. Hosts can cancel without fees or other adverse consequences under the policy. Eligibility depends on event, location, timing, and impact.
