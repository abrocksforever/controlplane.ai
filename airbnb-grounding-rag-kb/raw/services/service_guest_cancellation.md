# What guests need to know about a service or experience cancellation policy

Official source: https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/1593
Retrieved: 2026-08-29

Cancellation policies vary by service or experience. Most allow full refund until 24 hours before start; select offerings allow 72 hours. Outside the free window, a refund is not automatic unless an exception applies or the host agrees. Major Disruptive Events and qualifying service/experience issues can override the normal policy. Poor weather alone is not generally a refund-qualifying issue unless it prevents the activity.
