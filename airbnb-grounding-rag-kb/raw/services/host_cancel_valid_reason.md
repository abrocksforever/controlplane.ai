# Canceling a reservation as a host without adverse consequences

Official source: https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/2022
Retrieved: 2026-08-29

A host who must cancel because of a valid reason that makes hosting impossible may have fees or other consequences waived. Examples include personal illness or major property damage. Documentation may be required and hosts should cancel promptly. Exact consequences depend on whether the reservation is a home, service, or experience.
