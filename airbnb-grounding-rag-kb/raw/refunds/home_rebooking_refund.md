# Rebooking and refund policy for homes

Official source: https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/2868
Retrieved: 2026-08-29

If a host cancels a home reservation before check-in, the guest is entitled to a full refund and Airbnb may help find a similar place depending on availability. Airbnb may convert the original payment to travel credit for immediate rebooking, but the guest can request the original payment method instead; unused travel credit is refunded after 72 hours. Reservation Issues generally must be reported within 72 hours of discovery. Covered issues include host cancellation, failure to provide access, uninhabitable accommodations, and material differences from the listing. This policy controls when it applies.
