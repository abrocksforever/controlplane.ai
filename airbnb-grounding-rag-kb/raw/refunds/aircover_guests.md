# AirCover for guests

Official source: https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/3218
Retrieved: 2026-08-29

Every home booking comes with AirCover for guests. It can help with serious issues such as host cancellation before check-in, inability to get help from the host, or a listing significantly different from advertised. Depending on availability and circumstances, Airbnb may rebook the guest or provide a full or partial refund, including service fees. AirCover does not guarantee a full refund for every complaint.
