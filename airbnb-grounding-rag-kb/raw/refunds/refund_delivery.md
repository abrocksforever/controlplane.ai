# How you'll get your refund

Official source: https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/987
Retrieved: 2026-08-29

Eligible refunds are issued to the original payment method. If the original account is closed, the guest may need to work with the bank. Airbnb says eligible refunds are initiated as soon as cancellation occurs, but receipt time depends on the bank or financial institution. Credits may sometimes be chosen instead of a refund to the original payment method.
